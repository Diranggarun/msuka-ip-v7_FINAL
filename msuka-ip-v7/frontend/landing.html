What MSUkaIP is for

MSUkaIP is a messaging and calling app for the College of Information and Computing Sciences. Everything runs on a computer here on campus, so students, faculty and staff can keep in touch even when the internet is down or slow.

  • Instant messaging

    Send messages, photos and files to anyone on the same network.

  • Voice calls

    Call someone right in your browser. There is nothing to install.

  • Works without internet

    It only needs the campus Wi-Fi, not an internet connection.

  • Private and secure

    Your messages stay on the campus server and are stored encrypted, in line with the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).

How to connect

Five steps, about two minutes. You only need to do this once per device.

  1. 1

    Join the campus Wi-Fi

    Connect your phone or laptop to the same Wi-Fi network as the MSUkaIP server.

    Show me
    Open your Wi-Fi settings and pick the network your instructor or lab gave you. If the campus Wi-Fi does not let devices see each other, use the phone hotspot your class set up for MSUkaIP instead.
  2. 2

    Open the server address

    Open your browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) and type this address:

    https://<server-address>:3443

    Show me
    The first time, the browser may warn that the connection is not private. That is because the campus server signs its own certificate. Choose Advanced, then Proceed. You can remove the warning for good by installing the campus certificate from this link.
  3. 3

    Sign in or create an account

    Use your institutional email. New accounts wait for an administrator to approve them.

    Show me
    Choose Create Account, enter your full name, your MSU email and a password of at least 8 characters. Once an administrator approves you, sign in with the same email and password.
  4. 4

    Allow the microphone

    When the browser asks to use your microphone, choose Allow. Calls and voice messages need it.

    Show me
    The question appears at the top of the browser the first time you start a call. If you pressed Block by mistake, tap the lock icon beside the address and turn the microphone back on.
  5. 5

    Start chatting or calling

    Pick someone from your list, or open CICS Chat to talk to everyone in CICS.

Troubleshooting

Quick answers to the problems people run into most.

Can't reach the server address?
Check that you are on the same Wi-Fi as the server, and that you typed the address exactly, including the numbers after the colon. If it still won't open, the campus Wi-Fi may be stopping devices from seeing each other; use the class hotspot instead.
Microphone not working?
Make sure the address starts with https://. Browsers only allow the microphone on secure addresses. Then tap the lock icon beside the address and set Microphone to Allow.
Call has no sound?
Check that both of you allowed the microphone, that your volume is up, and that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network. Hanging up and calling again usually fixes it.
Do I need internet?
No. MSUkaIP only needs the campus Wi-Fi. Messages and calls never leave the campus network.
Is my data private?
Yes. Messages and files are stored encrypted on the campus server. Administrators can see account and sign-in activity for security, but they cannot read your messages. This follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
