Welcome, user.

Before you continue, please read and accept the following terms and conditions.

1. Institutional Use Only

This system is for official communication within the College of Information and Computing Sciences. Access is limited to verified MSU institutional accounts.

2. Privacy (RA 10173)

Your messages and files are encrypted at rest. Administrators can see account and login activity for security, but cannot read your message contents.

3. Monitoring

Login attempts and security-relevant actions are recorded in an audit log for accountability.

4. Respectful Conduct

Harassment, spam, and sharing of offensive or unlawful content are prohibited and may lead to account suspension.

5. No Misuse

Attempting to bypass authentication, access other accounts, or disrupt the service is a violation of this agreement.

Your security and privacy are our priority.
